E ? B: Parallel Plate Velocity Selector

H1 PhysicsH2 Physics By Lim Hwee Ke

A uniform electric field (E = 1500 V/m, downward between parallel plates) and a magnetic field (B = 0.5 mT, into the page) act perpendicularly on an electron entering from the left. The electric force qE acts upward on the electron; the magnetic force Bqv acts downward. Select from three entry speeds: at v = E/B = 3.0 Mm/s the forces balance and the electron passes straight through; at v = 6.0 Mm/s the magnetic force dominates and curves it toward the negative plate; at v = 1.2 Mm/s the electric force dominates and curves it toward the positive plate.
